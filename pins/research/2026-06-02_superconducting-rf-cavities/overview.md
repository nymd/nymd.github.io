# Superconducting RF Cavities: Nb3Sn Q-factor Tests

**Started:** 2026-06-02
**Status:** Testing C-04

## Goal
Compare the quality factor (Q0) of Nb3Sn-coated 1.3 GHz cavities against bare niobium at 4.2 K.

## Cavities under test
| ID | Coating | Q0 at 10 MV/m |
|---|---|---|
| C-01 | Bare Nb | 2.1e9 |
| C-02 | Nb3Sn | 1.8e10 |
| C-03 | Nb3Sn | 1.5e10 |
| C-04 | Nb3Sn (re-coated) | 1.9e10 |

## Open questions
- Why does C-03 quench at 14 MV/m when C-02 reaches 18 MV/m?
- Can a second re-coating push C-04 past 20 MV/m?
- Is the C-01 baseline still valid after the cryostat was refurbished?
- Should C-02 be re-tested at 2 K for comparison?
