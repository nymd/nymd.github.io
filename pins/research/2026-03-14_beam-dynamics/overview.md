# Beam Dynamics: Emittance Growth Study

**Started:** 2026-03-14
**Status:** Analysis complete

## Goal
Measure how horizontal emittance grows over 10,000 turns in the test ring when intra-beam scattering dominates.

## Setup
- Beam energy: 3.0 GeV
- Bunch charge: 1.2 nC
- Lattice: double-bend achromat, 24 cells

## Summary
Emittance grew by about 14% over the run, within 2% of the simulation's prediction.

## Follow-up (2026-11-19)
Repeat run with bunch charge lowered to 0.8 nC showed 9% growth, consistent with the IBS scaling.

## Next steps
Run the 0.8 nC case at 2.5 GeV to separate energy and charge effects.
Also scan the RF voltage from 1.6 to 2.0 MV to test bunch-lengthening effects.
Write up results for the December group meeting.
